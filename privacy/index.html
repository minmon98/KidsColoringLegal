Privacy Policy

Kids Coloring is designed to work without a child account, profile, advertising identifier, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or third-party advertising SDK.

Summary

Kids Coloring is a children's coloring app. The app is designed to work without a child account, profile, advertising identifier, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or third-party advertising SDK.

Data We Handle

Data Purpose Stored Where Shared With
Coloring strokes and saved drawings Resume artwork and show Gallery On device only Not shared by the app
Local settings Sound, haptics, onboarding state On device only Not shared by the app
Artwork cache Load thumbnails and line art faster On device only Not shared by the app
Purchase entitlement state Unlock Premium content Apple StoreKit and minimal local app state Apple processes purchases
Photo export Save a parent-approved picture Photos library after permission Controlled by the user/device

Children

The app does not ask children for names, email addresses, birthdays, exact ages, photos, audio, location, or account credentials. Purchases, external links, and exports are placed behind a parental gate.

Purchases

Premium purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple through StoreKit. The app checks verified entitlement status to unlock Premium content. Restore Purchases is available in Parent Area. Subscription terms are available in the Terms of Use (EULA).

Support

Support requests should be made by an adult. Support communications may include information the adult chooses to provide, such as purchase troubleshooting details.

Retention And Controls

Saved drawings, settings, and artwork cache remain on the device until the parent resets local data, clears cache, or deletes the app. Resetting local data does not remove App Store purchase entitlement.
