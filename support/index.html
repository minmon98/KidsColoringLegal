Support

Help for purchases, privacy, saved artwork, export, and Parent Area controls.

Before You Contact Us

  • For Premium access, open Parent Area and use Restore Purchases.
  • For subscriptions, open Parent Area and choose Manage Subscription.
  • For refunds, use Apple's refund page at https://reportaproblem.apple.com/.
  • For local storage concerns, use Clear Artwork Cache or Reset Local Data in Parent Area.

What To Include

  • Your device model and iOS or iPadOS version.
  • The app version and whether the issue happens on iPhone, iPad, or both.
  • For purchase issues, the product type: monthly, yearly, or lifetime.
  • A short description of what you tried and what happened.

Privacy Reminder

Support should be handled by an adult. Please do not send children's names, exact ages, birthdays, private artwork, location, photos, audio, or account credentials.
